This is my growing collection of website research: what I observed, what public source supports, and what I would take into a new design.

Each website has its own document, with its research date, screenshots, typography, interactions, source evidence, and limitations together. The shared findings have a separate page that connects the observations and explains what they mean.

## Read the shared findings

[Research findings](/docs/research-findings/) brings together the creative directions, recurring patterns, typography measurements, responsive comparisons, and recommendations from the September 15–16, 2026 research. It also explains the public-source evidence and the scope of 65 additional route requests.

Use it to compare the websites and understand the lessons across them. Follow the links to each website for the detailed observations and original screenshots.

## How to read the findings

An observed interaction, a source configuration, and a design interpretation support different kinds of conclusions. The [methodology](/docs/methodology/) explains those distinctions and the limits of the current research.

Live websites change. These reports describe the sites during their recorded research sessions; an old finding is not a claim about the current version.
